Find your happy place, piece by piece.

Piecepop is a colorful jigsaw puzzle game made for relaxed moments. Choose an illustration, set your challenge, and enjoy the satisfying feeling of pieces snapping into place. Play at your own pace with no timer, no ads, and no account to create.

44 PUZZLES, 11 CATEGORIES
Explore nature, animals, art, places, fantasy, food, space, dinosaurs, festivals, cozy rooms, and architecture. From alpine lakes and lantern rivers to dinosaur adventures and a picnic near Saturn, there is a picture for every mood.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY EACH PICTURE
Choose 36, 100, 144, 256, or 400 pieces. Start with a quick puzzle or settle into a bigger challenge. Zoom in when you want a closer look at the details.

A LITTLE HELP WHEN YOU WANT IT
Drag pieces from the scrolling tray, snap matching neighbors together, and move connected groups. Use hints, an edge-piece filter, a reference picture, and four workspace backgrounds to make your puzzle time your own.

DAILY PICKS AND MYSTERY MODE
Discover a daily pick from the included free collection, rotating by your device's date. Try Mystery mode to hide the reference picture and discover the scene as you assemble it.

EARN YOUR NEXT LITTLE ESCAPE
Start with 28 free puzzles and earn virtual coins by completing pictures. Spend those earned coins to unlock 16 more puzzles. There are no real-money purchases.

OFFLINE AND READY WHEN YOU ARE
All 44 illustrations are included in the download. Enjoy puzzles without an Internet connection. Progress saves automatically on your device so you can return to an unfinished picture later.

YOUR MOMENT, YOUR PACE
Enjoy cheerful artwork, gentle sound effects, optional haptics, and a colorful celebration when your picture is complete.
