Piecepop Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 12, 2026

Developer: Akhilesh Mahto
Contact: akhileshedd@gmail.com
Android package: com.akhileshedd.piecepop

1. About this policy
This policy describes the Android version of Piecepop published by Akhilesh Mahto. Piecepop is an offline jigsaw puzzle game. It does not require an account.

2. Information collected by the app
Piecepop does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or third-party account sign-in. The Android app does not request Internet access, location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions.

3. Information stored on your device
Piecepop saves puzzle progress, completed puzzles, earned virtual coins, unlocked puzzles, and sound, haptic, and background preferences in the app's private storage on your device. This information is used only to provide the game and resume your progress. Virtual coins are earned through play and have no monetary value.

The app uses Android's vibration permission only for optional haptic feedback. Vibration does not collect or transmit information, and you can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The app does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server or provide a cloud-sync service. Android application backup is disabled in this release. Your operating system or device manufacturer may separately provide device-transfer or other system services under its own policies.

4. Deleting local progress
You can delete Piecepop's local information by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. This also removes saved puzzles, coins, unlocks, and preferences. The developer cannot restore information that exists only on your device.

5. Support communications
If you choose to email akhileshedd@gmail.com, your email address and the information you include will be received through the developer's email provider. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Support correspondence is kept only as needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

6. Sharing and third-party services
The app does not sell or share personal information and does not send gameplay information to third parties. Google Play and your device's operating system may process information independently when you download, update, or use your device. Their services are governed by their own privacy policies. Email support is handled outside the app.

7. Children
Piecepop does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support email, contact the developer to request its deletion.

8. Security
Local game saves use the app's private device storage. Protect your device using the security features provided by Android. No method of storage or email communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes
This policy may be updated if the app's behavior or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will show a new effective date and will be made available with the app and its store listing.

10. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact Akhilesh Mahto at akhileshedd@gmail.com.
