# Original puzzle artwork

All 18 square SVG illustrations in `assets/puzzles` were created specifically for this project. They contain original vector paths and shapes and do not incorporate third-party photographs, logos, stock illustrations, or game assets. The unused original `alpine_escape.svg` remains available alongside the featured painted version.

- `alpine_escape.svg` — a sunlit mountain lake, chalet, rowing boat, and wildflower meadow.
- `coral_cove.svg` — a sea turtle and tropical fish swimming above a colorful coral reef.
- `citrus_sunshine.svg` — Mediterranean citrus, patterned ceramics, and a seaside window.
- `moonlight_meadow.svg` — a cozy cottage and flowers beside a moonlit stream.
- `toucan_tropics.svg` — an expressive toucan among tropical foliage and hibiscus.
- `candy_coast.svg` — a colorful seaside village, sailing boats, and striped beach umbrellas.
- `cactus_carnival.svg` — desert saguaros, rock formations, hot-air balloons, and flowering cacti.
- `waterfall_wonder.svg` — a jungle waterfall, rainbow, toucans, ferns, and tropical flowers.
- `sunflower_fields.svg` — a sunflower farm, red barn, windmill, and winding country path.
- `autumn_river.svg` — golden and scarlet trees beside a river, bridge, and cottage.
- `lighthouse_bay.svg` — a striped lighthouse, rocky coast, fishing boats, and wildflowers.
- `lavender_hills.svg` — purple lavender rows, a farmhouse, bicycle, and warm afternoon light.
- `panda_picnic.svg` — a panda picnic among bamboo, lanterns, and riverside flowers.
